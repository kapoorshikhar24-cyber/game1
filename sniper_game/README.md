# ELITE SNIPER: SHADOWS OF INDIA

**Genre:** Cinematic Military Reconnaissance Thriller  
**Setting:** Fictional Himalayan Border Sector, Northern India  
**Protagonist:** Captain Arjun Rathore (Reconnaissance & Precision Operations Specialist)

---

## 🎯 Core Creative Direction & Design Philosophy

> **Core Loop:** Observe → Investigate → Decide → Act → Deal with Consequences → Extract

The player is a **military reconnaissance operator first and a sniper second**.

### The Golden Design Rule
> **Never give the player a shooting objective when an observation or investigation objective would create better gameplay.**

Instead of generic `KILL 5 ENEMIES` waypoints, objectives are grounded in observation and intelligence gathering:
- **OBSERVE THE COMPOUND:** Determine who is coordinating the activity.
- **INVESTIGATE PATROL VEHICLE:** Examine environmental clues to uncover unauthorized communications networks.
- **TACTICAL DECISION:** Choose whether to take the shot, observe, follow, or extract.

---

## 🏔️ What Makes The Game Different

1. **Reconnaissance & Observation:**
   - Use binoculars and mil-dot scopes to survey terrain, track patrol patterns, and identify targets based on physical profiles rather than red UI markers.
2. **Investigation & Environmental Clues:**
   - Inspect abandoned vehicles, damaged radios, field recorders, encrypted sub-station terminals, and signal logs.
3. **Stealth & Tactical Positioning:**
   - Choose routes, natural mountain concealment, elevation, and timing over direct confrontation.
4. **Physically-Based Ballistics & Bullet Management:**
   - Realistic gravity drop, wind drift, breath control sway, and realistic muzzle flash visibility. Ammunition and shot placement carry high tactical stakes.
5. **Decision-Based Narrative Branching:**
   - Tactical choices (e.g. *Shoot Kabir* vs *Observe Kabir & Tara*) dynamically alter future mission briefings, story beats, and character interactions across all 10 campaign chapters.
6. **Intelligent AI Reaction:**
   - Enemy guards investigate suspicious sounds, spot unsuppressed shots, raise sector alert levels, and investigate unassigned tracks.
7. **Authentic Indian Military Setting:**
   - High-altitude Himalayan sectors, severe whiteout blizzards, dense northeastern monsoons, mountain communications infrastructure, and rich military atmosphere.
8. **Cinematic Presentation:**
   - Tactical tablet UI, encrypted radio communications (Major Meera Sen, Kabir, Tara), atmospheric weather shaders, and PBR environments built in Three.js.

## 📜 10-Mission Campaign Structure

1. **Mission 01 — Operation Trishul Shadow** *(Himalayan Border Sector)*: Observation ridge, target confirmation, Kabir & Tara encounter.
2. **Mission 02 — The Lost Patrol** *(Mountain Supply Route)*: Environmental tracking, damaged field recorder recovery.
3. **Mission 03 — Echo Under Snow** *(Abandoned Comms Station)*: Terminal decryption, internal command leak discovery.
4. **Mission 04 — Whiteout** *(High-Altitude Mountain Pass)*: Whiteout storm survival, shadow operator tracking.
5. **Mission 05 — The Informant** *(Remote Mountain Village)*: Tara confrontation, decision to extract or follow to Kabir.
6. **Mission 06 — Broken Chain** *(Mountain Intelligence Facility)*: Base archive infiltration, Meera's off-record orders.
7. **Mission 07 — The Shadow Network** *(Remote Mountain Infrastructure)*: Dual-objective sniper overwatch with Kabir.
8. **Mission 08 — The Last Signal** *(High-Altitude Comms Complex)*: 04:00 countdown, choice to destroy or trace core source.
9. **Mission 09 — Trishul** *(Mountain Command HQ)*: Internal mastermind reveal, choice of public exposure vs silent wipeout.
10. **Mission 10 — Shadows of India** *(Himalayan Command Sector)*: Unguided target deduction, precision shot, and full epilogue.

## 🎮 Controls

- **WASD**: Movement
- **Mouse**: Look / Aim
- **Right Click (Hold/Toggle)**: Aim Down Sights (Scope)
- **Left Click**: Fire Weapon
- **Shift (Hold)**: Steady Scope (Breath Control)
- **B**: Toggle Binoculars / Recon Scanner
- **Tab / T**: Open Tactical Tablet & Intelligence Dossier
- **Q**: Switch Weapon / Suppressor Toggle
